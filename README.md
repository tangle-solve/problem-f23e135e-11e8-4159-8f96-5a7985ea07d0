### ALGO-000067: Unconditional NP-hardness of total MCSP
- **Subfield:** Proof complexity and metacomplexity
- **Open:** Prove that the Minimum Circuit Size Problem for complete truth tables is NP-hard under deterministic polynomial-time many-one reductions.
- **Status:** Open since the 1950s–1970s origins of circuit minimization. Partial-function MCSP and conditional hardness results do not settle total MCSP under this reduction.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Would explain the apparent intractability of recognizing computational structure in explicit data.
- **Key works:** Hirahara (2022), partial-MCSP hardness; Hirahara and Ilango (2025), *NP-Hardness of the Minimum Circuit Size Problem from Well-Studied Assumptions*, DOI: 10.1109/FOCS63196.2025.00087.
- **Related:** Machine learning—distinguishing structure from randomness.
